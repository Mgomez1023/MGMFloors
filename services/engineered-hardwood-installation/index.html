Engineered Hardwood Installation in Chicago

Engineered hardwood combines a real-wood surface with a layered core, creating options for projects where product construction and installation conditions matter.

Service Overview

Choose the board and installation as one system

Engineered products vary in thickness, core construction, wear layer, finish, width, and manufacturer requirements.

MGM considers the selected board alongside the subfloor, finished height, adjoining rooms, and approved installation method before work begins.

Where engineered hardwood may fit

  • Projects calling for a factory-finished real-wood surface
  • Locations where the approved installation method differs from traditional solid wood
  • Renovations with important finished-height constraints
  • Wide-plank designs supported by a selected product
  • Spaces where subfloor and site conditions have been reviewed

The MGM Approach

A five-step approach

The product instructions, project conditions, and desired room-to-room flow guide each stage.

  1. 01

    Review the site

    Check the rooms, subfloor type, access, floor heights, and transitions.

  2. 02

    Confirm the board

    Review construction, dimensions, finish, installation requirements, and visual variation.

  3. 03

    Plan the layout

    Set direction, starting lines, joint distribution, and key sightlines.

  4. 04

    Install as approved

    Use an installation approach appropriate to the selected product and site conditions.

  5. 05

    Finish the details

    Resolve perimeters, vents, thresholds, and connections to adjoining surfaces.

Service Considerations

Product construction and installation methods

“Engineered” describes a category, not one interchangeable specification.

Board construction

Core type, overall thickness, wear layer, edge profile, and finish differ by product and affect selection and future options.

Site and subfloor conditions

Concrete or wood substrates, flatness, moisture conditions, floor height, and manufacturer instructions influence preparation and installation.

Project Details

Details to confirm before ordering

Early coordination reduces surprises at doors, stairs, cabinets, and adjacent flooring.

Approved method
Products may allow specific fastening, adhesive, or floating methods under defined conditions.
Subfloor readiness
The supporting surface must meet the selected product’s requirements.
Board variation
Color and grain should be reviewed across multiple cartons during layout.
Finished height
Doors, appliances, stairs, and adjacent floors may be affected.
Transitions
Profiles and locations should be planned with the surrounding interior.
