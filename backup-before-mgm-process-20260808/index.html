Our Story

Built on Craft,
Driven by Pride

MGM Floors LLC was founded on a simple belief: every home and business deserves floors that are both beautiful and built to last. Based in the Chicago area, we bring old-world craftsmanship to every project — whether it's a single-room hardwood install or a full commercial build-out.

Our team of licensed professionals brings decades of combined experience across hardwood, luxury vinyl, epoxy, and tile. We source premium materials, use industry-leading techniques, and never cut corners — because your floors are the foundation of everything.
